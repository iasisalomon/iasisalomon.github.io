## The challenge

New-client onboarding required repeated manual setup and follow-up. Each onboarding took an estimated 30 minutes of manual work, and failures needed a clear path to a person.

## My role

I owned the design, delivery, and maintenance of operational workflows end to end. Across this work I used Rewst, n8n, Retool, and Make.

## The workflow

- A support ticket starts the onboarding workflow.
- The workflow provisions the new user's Google Workspace account and notifies both support and the client.
- Failed steps retry automatically; unresolved failures escalate to a person.

## Outcome

The automated flow saves an estimated **30 minutes per onboarding** while keeping exceptions visible and recoverable by the support team.
